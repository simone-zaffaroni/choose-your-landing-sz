Privacy notice

OUTDIRT · EICMA 2026 landing page · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This notice explains how personal data is processed on this page, under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Italian Privacy Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003).

Data controller

Red S.r.l., Via dell'Unione 3, Milano (Italy). E-mail: redazione@red-live.it. For anything about this page you can also write to simone@getdirt.it.

What we collect

  • Through the DMD form: name, company, e-mail address, your message, and whether you asked to receive updates.
  • When you browse: the technical data any website receives (IP address, browser, date and time of the request), handled by our hosting provider to deliver and protect the page.

This page does not use its own analytics, advertising or profiling cookies.

Why, and on what legal basis

  • Answering your request (information about DMD, OUTDIRT and its brands, a meeting, a proposal): pre-contractual measures taken at your request, art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Sending you DMD and OUTDIRT news, only if you tick the optional box: your consent, art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time, with a link in each message or by e-mail.
  • Running the website securely: our legitimate interest, art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Name and e-mail are needed to answer you; without them we cannot reply to the form.

Who receives the data

The people in the OUTDIRT group (DIRT Digital Agency, BLACKOUT, GetDIRT) who handle your request, and the providers that run the page for us, acting as processors: the hosting provider (Netlify, Inc. or GitHub, Inc.), Web3Forms (which delivers the form to us by e-mail) and Google (web fonts, which receives your IP address when the fonts load). Data is not sold or disclosed to anyone else.

Third-party content

Some sections show posts and reels from Instagram (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) through Instagram's own embeds. When they load, Meta may receive data about your visit and may set its own cookies, under its own policy: Instagram privacy policy. Links to Instagram, TikTok and YouTube take you to those services, which apply their own policies.

Transfers outside the EU

Some providers may process data in the United States. Where this happens it is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Form requests: for as long as needed to follow up, and no longer than 24 months after the last contact.
  • Updates: until you withdraw your consent.
  • Technical data: for the short periods set by the hosting provider for security purposes.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format (arts. 15–22 GDPR), and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to redazione@red-live.it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
